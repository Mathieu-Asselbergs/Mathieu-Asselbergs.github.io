Preferences

Hi there! Thank you for your interest in my wishlist. Please keep the following few things in mind when getting me something to ensure that you don't get me something I wouldn't like.


Please stick to the list. I don't like tossing gifts (Read more...)

Below is a curated list of things I would like to receive. Items that are not featured on this list may not be appreciated; get me these at your own risk. I do not like tossing gifts I've received, but unwanted gifts qualify for tossing when I want to get rid of things... Getting me non-physical gifts, or gifts with an expiration date would then be nicer. I will only ever need to toss these in case of expiration. This is less of an issue for me. An exception is made for self-made physical gifts with a personal nature and a compact form factor (i.e. a letter, card, puzzle, CD, DVD, (collection of) computer file(s), etc.)

Please don't put me to work on my own... (Read more...)

I do not much appreciate gifts that require me to spend time doing an activity individually. I have a hard enough time trying to balance a student life in Eindhoven and a social life in Eindhoven('t). However nice your thought is, there is a good chance I would prefer to spend your gift's required time with you.

Wanna chip in for a BIG gift? (Read more...)

For all of these gifts, it holds that I also appreciate receiving it partially. I consider as a partial gift:

  • a component of a gift that is on the list,
  • money, if and only if it is clearly attributed to a specific gift on the list, or
  • a letter, voice memo, webpage, picture, or other form of communication that promises the later gifting of something on the list or the partial version of something on the list.
Examples of partial gifts would be:
  • the promise of a movie ticket for when something fun hits the cinema,
  • a promise of a financial contribution for a gift on the list (such as a concert ticket),
  • money that you mention is clearly reserved for a specific activity/item,
  • a promise of a nice snacc/drincc for something like a movie night later on,
  • etc.


In short, if my wishlist and what you want to give me don't align, I would rather receive nothing than something I don't like. I appreciate your presence more than I could appreciate any one gift, and you listening to and understanding my wishes is a big gift to give me, too! Thank you for taking the time to understand my gift preferences. I appreciate it :)


P.S. I do my best to not include store links here, so that you do not expect that I found the best deal. I didn't. Some products have no non-store page, though... If you click a store page, please know that there may be others as well! Perhaps with better deals!
